## The email

**From:** Mateo Chen, Owner, Side Quest Coffee Co.
**Subject:** Website stuff for Side Quest

Hi!

Thanks again for helping me with this. Everyone keeps telling me I need "a real website," which I suppose is true. Right now people mostly hear about us from friends or walk past the shop, and that's probably not enough anymore.

I don't really know what a website is supposed to have, so I'm trusting you on the tech part. You're the tech-savvy one.

The main thing I want people to understand is that **Side Quest Coffee Co. is an actual neighborhood coffee shop**, but tabletop games are a big part of why people stay here. We have shelves of board games, cards, dice, chess sets, that kind of thing. People will come in intending to grab coffee and somehow still be here two hours later playing something with people they just met.

That's kind of the Side Quest idea: you were on your way somewhere else and ended up having an unexpectedly good afternoon.

I definitely want the place to feel fun and a little quirky, but not childish. We get plenty of students and game groups, but we also have remote workers, neighborhood regulars, parents, and people who stop in for meetings. I don't want somebody meeting a prospective client here to feel like they've walked into a toy store.

At the same time, please don't make us look like Generic Corporate Coffee Company #847. We're not that either.

The actual shop has warm wood tables, mismatched chairs that somehow work together, plants in the windows, shelves of games, and a big community table in the middle. There are always cards and game pieces around. One thing I like about tabletop games is that people actually look at each other instead of another screen for a while.

We serve coffee, obviously. Some of the things people order most are:

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Drip coffee | $3.25 |
| Cold brew | $4.50 |
| Maple latte | $5.25 |
| Chai latte | $4.75 |
| Cinnamon roll | $4.00 |
| Egg and cheddar breakfast sandwich | $6.50 |
| Soft pretzel with mustard | $5.50 |

You don't necessarily have to put every food item front and center. I just want visitors to understand that this is a coffee shop and that they can actually get food and drinks here.

People also ask about our events all the time.

**Open Table Thursday** happens every Thursday from **6:30 to 9:30 PM**. People can bring their own games or grab something from our shelves, and it's fine to come alone. That's probably our biggest regular event.

**Chess & Coffee** is every Sunday from **11:00 AM to 1:00 PM**. It's casual. You absolutely do not have to be good at chess.

On the **first Wednesday of each month**, we do a **Learn-a-Game Night from 7:00 to 8:30 PM**. Someone from the staff picks a game and teaches it. It's especially good for people who like the idea of tabletop games but don't know where to start.

Eventually I'd love to let people sign up for events online. I also keep thinking it would be cool if someone could search our entire game collection and see whether a particular game is on the shelf right now. Maybe someday.

**Please do not turn this website project into that project.** Right now I need a straightforward website people can visit to understand who we are and decide whether they want to stop by. Online ordering would also be nice eventually, but again: not now.
