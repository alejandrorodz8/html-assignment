Mateo's café brings together warm wood tables, mismatched chairs that work together, plants in the windows, and shelves of tabletop games. At the heart of it all is a big community table—a place to gather over a game of cards.

Come with your game group, meet a neighbor, or settle in with a coffee. Students, remote workers, parents, neighborhood regulars, and people meeting clients all have a place here.
